The first stage of sampling involves the definition,
stratification, and selection of primary sampling units
(PSU), which are defined as large metropolitan areas,
counties, or groups of counties. The second stage of
sampling occurs at the housing unit level. Within
the PSUs selected, the sampling process identifies
addresses to be included in the sample, and interviews
are conducted with persons and households at
those addresses.
